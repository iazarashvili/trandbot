The structural stop was a good idea that lost

Placing the stop where the setup is invalidated — behind the swing the shift broke, or behind the zone — is the textbook answer and it is measurably worse here. The honest size of this result

28 trades. If every trade were a coin with the 29% win chance that a 1:2.5 target needs to break even, you would see 10 wins or better 26% of the time by luck alone. The one statistically solid number in this whole report is the 389-trade ablation run — and it says the 1m trigger by itself is worth −0.08R.

The choice of 1:2.5 in particular is not supported: the standard error on expectancy here is about ±0.35R, so every ratio in the sweep sits inside one error bar of every other. Treat the configuration as a hypothesis to test forward, not a result.
